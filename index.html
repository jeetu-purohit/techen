About the Firm

A decade of reliable supply, built from Parrys.

Founded in 2015 in Parrys — Chennai's historic wholesale trading quarter — Techen Electricals has grown into a trusted name for distribution boards, MCBs, LED indicators, aluminium lugs and the everyday components that keep electrical installations running.

As an authorized stockist of Powermat Conicals, PVC Truff, MCB Channel and Panel Locks, we supply contractors, retailers and electricians across the city with genuine, reasonably priced stock — delivered on schedule, every time.
